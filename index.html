Track 01 — Virtual Events

Webinars, simulives & virtual chats

Full lifecycle for CP2E and Pro virtual events. Use the Virtual Event Checklist below as the standard template. Steps are shared across teams — team-specific callouts are noted inline where workflows differ.

Event Frequency & Timing

Avoid: Winter holidays (late Nov–Jan), early summer (June–July), spring break, and any major Intuit company-wide event weeks — attendance drops significantly.
Best windows: Sept–Oct and Feb–Apr tend to perform strongest for both CP2E and Pro events.
Spacing: Allow at least 2–3 weeks between events targeting the same audience to avoid list fatigue.
Timing: Mid-week (Tue–Thu), 8am–12pm PT performs best across both teams based on historical data.

Choose Your Event Format
Live webinar, simulive, or on-demand — pick the right format before building anything
+
Live Webinar
Real-time interaction with panelists and attendees. Best for Q&A-heavy events, new audiences, or high-conversion goals. Requires full production support.
Best for: one-time webinar, high-engagement, high-intent audiences
Simulive
Pre-recorded content played live with a live host moderating chat/Q&A. Reduces speaker dependency while keeping audience engagement. CP2E primary format.
Best for: recurring series, cost efficiency, consistent delivery
On-Demand
A Zoom cloud recording converted to on-demand access. Registrants receive a link to watch the recording at any time — no scheduled broadcast, no live host. Self-serve replay with no additional production required.
Best for: post-event replay, audiences who missed the live session, evergreen content, always-on lead capture

Feedback & continuous improvement

Go beyond SurveyMonkey. Use multiple feedback channels to build a complete picture of what worked and what to improve.

SurveyMonkey
Primary post-event feedback tool. Captures quantitative NPS and qualitative comments from attendees. Send within 24 hours of event close.
Speaker debrief
Informal Slack or quick call with panelists within 1–2 days. Ask: what went well, what felt off, any audience reactions to note. Send spotlight as a thank you.
Audience interviews (FMH)
Conduct periodic 1:1 interviews with target audience members for deeper qualitative insight — not after every event, but at least once per series or quarter.

Governance & DACE

Roles, responsibilities & event intake

Who does what — and how TM decides whether to lead, support, or decline an event request. Use the DACE tables to distribute work before the first planning meeting.

Step 1 — Go/No-Go readiness check

Run this check before any planning begins. All four criteria must be GO or WARNING to proceed. A single NO-GO is a hard stop — resolve it or postpone.

Criteria ✓ GO ⚠ WARNING ✗ NO-GO
Lead Time 6+ weeks out 4 weeks out <3 weeks out
Staffing Named coordinator committed Tentative / TBD No coordinator identified
Value Prop Clear hiring goal attached Generic / vague No benefit defined
Budget Fully approved Pending approval Not identified
Golden Rules — mandatory for every event
  • → Every event must have one named Lead Coordinator — if none exists 7 days out, postpone
  • → A TA representative must be present at every event (no exceptions)
  • → Speaker confirmed at least 14 days before the event date
  • → T+24 hrs: Send thank you email + NPS survey to all attendees
  • → T+48 hrs: Recruiter high-touch outreach to all Senior-level attendees

Step 2 — Event intake criteria

TM RoleCriteria to qualifyTM contribution
Tier 1 — Lead High impact on sweet spot hiring goals + high % diversity talent + TA team identified to support Full planning ownership: event-specific email campaigns, lead capture, brand lift survey, full results reporting
Tier 2 — Support High impact on hiring goals OR high % diversity talent, with a recruiter identified Planning support: attend key meetings, standard lead capture form, generic post-event qualification campaign
Tier 3 — Resource only Early-career or broader talent mix; low/no hiring goal attached Deliver event-in-a-box: iCIMS lead form setup guide, generic talent community catch-all campaign (sent quarterly)
Decline No hiring priority alignment, no TA partner identified, or capacity is at limit No contribution — refer to Danna's advisory guide for escalation path

Contingency quick reference

Common risk scenarios and their standard pivot actions. Decide your response before event day — not during it.

RiskTriggerAction
Low registration <25% of target, 2 weeks out Internal referral blitz — or shift budget to LinkedIn paid push ($500–$1K)
Speaker drop-out Any time before event Evergreen swap: use best-of recording + live recruiter Q&A. Or pivot to fireside chat format.
Missing coordinator 7 days out, no one confirmed Hard stop — postpone the event. Do not proceed without a named coordinator.
Tech failure (day-of) Platform down or critical AV failure Encore protocol: send recording within 2 hrs, or reschedule within same week.
Low NPS Post-event score <30 Attendee recovery call + full strategy audit before scheduling next event in series.
